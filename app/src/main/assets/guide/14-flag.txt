# pledge | Pledge of Allegiance | Words and how to stand
I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one Nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.
- In uniform: face the flag and give the Scout salute.
- Not in uniform: place your right hand over your heart. Remove your hat with your right hand and hold it over your heart.

# flag-etiquette | Flag Etiquette | Respect the flag
- Raise the flag briskly. Lower it slowly and with ceremony.
- Fly it from sunrise to sunset. If it flies at night, it must be lit.
- Do not let the flag touch the ground.
- Do not fly it in bad weather unless it is an all-weather flag.
- When flown with other flags, the U.S. flag is highest or on its own right (the viewer's left).
- When displayed flat on a wall, the stars (the union) are at the top, on the flag's own right, the viewer's left.
## Half-staff
To show mourning, first raise the flag to the top, then lower it to halfway. Before taking it down for the day, raise it to the top again, then lower it.

# flag-folding | Folding the Flag | The triangle fold
This takes two people.
1. Fold the flag in half lengthwise, stripes over stars.
2. Fold it in half lengthwise again, keeping the blue field on the outside.
3. Starting at the striped end, make a triangle fold by bringing the corner up to meet the top edge.
4. Keep folding in triangles all the way down the flag.
5. Tuck the remaining edge into the folds. Only the blue field with stars should show.

# flag-ceremony | Flag Ceremonies | Opening and closing
## Opening ceremony
1. Leader: "Troop, attention." Everyone stands still.
2. "Color guard, advance." The color guard brings the flag forward.
3. "Scout salute." Everyone salutes.
4. "Please join in the Pledge of Allegiance."
5. "Two." Hands come down.
6. "Color guard, post the colors." The guard places the flag in its stand.
7. "Color guard, dismissed."
## Retiring a worn flag
A flag that is worn out should be retired in a dignified way, usually by burning in a respectful ceremony. Many troops hold flag retirement ceremonies as a service to the community.

# citizenship | Good Citizenship | Duty to country
- Know your community leaders and how government works.
- Volunteer and do service projects.
- Respect the rights and opinions of others.
- Obey the law and be informed about issues.
- When you are old enough, vote.
