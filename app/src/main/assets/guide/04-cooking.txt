=== cooking | Camp Cooking | 🍳 | #C26A1C | Menus, food safety, stoves and recipes

# meal-planning | Meal Planning | Plan menus as a patrol
1. Decide on the meals: how many breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and snacks.
2. Pick a balanced menu with protein, grains, fruit, vegetables, and dairy.
3. Check for allergies and dietary needs in your patrol.
4. Write a shopping list with exact quantities for the number of people.
5. Figure out the cost per Scout and stay within budget.
6. Plan the cooking gear and the cooking method for each meal.
> For backpacking, choose light, fast-cooking foods: instant rice, pasta, oatmeal, dried fruit, and foil-packed tuna or chicken.

# food-safety | Food Safety | Keep food from making you sick
- Wash hands before handling food.
- Keep cold food cold, below 40°F, in a cooler with ice. Keep hot food hot.
- Keep raw meat separate from other foods. Use a separate cutting board and utensils.
- Cook meat all the way through: ground beef to 160°F, chicken to 165°F.
- Do not leave food sitting out for more than 2 hours, or 1 hour in hot weather.
- When in doubt, throw it out.
! In bear country, store all food and scented items in a bear canister, bear box, or a proper bear hang.

# stoves | Stoves and Fuel Safety | Camp stoves the safe way
1. Only use a stove with adult supervision and after you have been trained.
2. Set the stove on a flat, stable surface clear of anything that can burn.
3. Check connections for leaks before lighting.
4. Light the match first, then turn on the fuel.
5. Never leave a lit stove unattended.
6. Let the stove cool before changing fuel canisters.
7. Store fuel away from the stove, tents, and fires.
! Never use a stove inside a tent or closed space.

# dutch-oven | Dutch Oven Basics | Baking with coals
A Dutch oven is a heavy cast-iron pot with a flat lid that holds coals.
- Use charcoal briquettes for even, predictable heat.
- To bake at about 350°F: take the diameter of the oven in inches. Put that number plus 3 on the lid, and that number minus 3 underneath. A 12-inch oven gets 15 on top and 9 below.
- Turn the oven a quarter turn one way and the lid a quarter turn the other every 15 minutes to avoid hot spots.
- Use a lid lifter and heavy gloves. Everything is very hot.
- Clean with hot water and a scraper, dry it over heat, and wipe with a thin coat of oil.

# cleanup | Dishwashing | The three-pot method
Set up three pots or tubs:
1. Hot wash water with a little biodegradable soap.
2. Hot rinse water.
3. Sanitizing rinse: very hot water or water with a few drops of bleach as directed.
Scrape food scraps into the trash first. Let dishes air dry on a clean surface or in a mesh bag.
## Gray water
Strain out food bits and pack them out with the trash. Scatter gray water broadly at least 200 feet from water and camp.

# recipes | Easy Camp Recipes | Patrol favorites
## Foil packet dinner (per Scout)
1. Tear off a large sheet of heavy-duty foil.
2. Add a hamburger patty or chicken pieces, sliced potato, carrot, onion, salt, and pepper. A pat of butter helps.
3. Fold it into a tight packet with double-folded edges.
4. Cook on hot coals 15 to 25 minutes, turning once. Check that meat is fully cooked.
## One-pot chili
1. Brown ground beef with chopped onion and drain the grease into a can.
2. Add canned beans, canned tomatoes, and chili seasoning.
3. Simmer 20 minutes, stirring often.
## Breakfast burritos
1. Cook sausage or bacon, then scramble eggs in the same pan.
2. Add cheese and salsa.
3. Wrap in warm tortillas.
## Banana boats (dessert)
1. Slit a banana lengthwise through the peel without cutting through the bottom.
2. Stuff with chocolate chips and mini marshmallows.
3. Wrap in foil and warm on coals 5 to 10 minutes.
