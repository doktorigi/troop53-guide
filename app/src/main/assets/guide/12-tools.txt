=== tools | Woods Tools | 🪓 | #6B4A3A | Knives, axes, saws and Totin' Chip

# knife-safety | Knife Safety | Totin' Chip rules
The Totin' Chip shows you know how to use woods tools safely. If you break the rules, a leader may cut a corner off your card. Lose all four and you lose the card.
- A knife is a tool, not a toy.
- Keep the blade closed when not in use. Close it with your palm, not your fingers.
- Always cut away from yourself and others.
- Make a safety circle: hold the closed knife at arm's length and turn around. If you can't touch anyone, it is safe to use.
- Pass a knife closed, or handle first. Say "thank you" when you receive it so the passer knows you have it.
- Keep it sharp. A dull knife slips and is more dangerous.
- Never throw a knife or carve on trees or tables.
- Keep it clean and dry.

# knife-sharpening | Sharpening a Knife | Use a whetstone
1. Put a few drops of oil or water on the stone, depending on the type.
2. Hold the blade at a low angle, about 20 degrees, against the stone.
3. Push the blade across the stone, edge first, as if slicing a thin layer off.
4. Do the same number of strokes on the other side.
5. Wipe the blade clean. Test on paper, not your thumb.

# axe-saw | Axe and Saw Safety | The axe yard
- Use an axe or saw only with training and permission.
- Work in an axe yard: an area roped off with a radius at least as long as the axe plus your arm. Only the user goes inside.
- Use a chopping block. Never chop directly on the ground.
- Wear sturdy shoes. Keep a firm stance with feet apart.
- Check that the axe head is tight and the handle has no cracks.
- Cover the blade with its sheath when not in use. Carry it by the handle with the blade down and to the side.
- With a saw, use long, steady strokes and keep your other hand clear of the blade.
! Never cut live trees. Use dead and down wood only.

# tool-care | Tool Care | Make tools last
- Clean and dry tools after every use.
- Oil metal parts lightly to prevent rust.
- Store sharp tools in sheaths or guards.
- Report broken tools to the quartermaster.
