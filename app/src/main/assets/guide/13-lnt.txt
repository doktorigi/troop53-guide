# lnt-principles | Seven Principles | Leave No Trace
1. Plan ahead and prepare: know the rules, the weather, and the area. Plan meals to cut down on trash.
2. Travel and camp on durable surfaces: stay on trails and use established campsites. Walk single file in the middle of the trail.
3. Dispose of waste properly: pack it in, pack it out. Use catholes 200 feet from water for human waste.
4. Leave what you find: leave rocks, plants, and historic items where they are. Take pictures, not souvenirs.
5. Minimize campfire impacts: use a stove or an existing fire ring. Keep fires small and burn wood to ash.
6. Respect wildlife: watch from a distance. Never feed animals. Store food securely.
7. Be considerate of other visitors: keep voices low, yield on trails, and let nature's sounds be heard.

# lnt-trash | Pack It In, Pack It Out | Trash and gear
- Bring trash bags and carry out all trash, including food scraps, peels, and wrappers.
- Pick up litter left by others. It's a great Good Turn.
- Repackage food at home to reduce trash on the trail.
- Check your campsite before leaving. Do a line sweep: walk the site in a line, shoulder to shoulder, picking up everything.
> Leave the campsite better than you found it.
