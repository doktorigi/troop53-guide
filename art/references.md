# Illustration references and review

Original artwork and reference comparison by Codex, 2026-10-12. No source photos, diagrams, logos, badges, or font files are redistributed. The editable SVG and Android XML paths are authored in this repository. AI is used only for the two camping scenes; exact prompts are in `image-prompts.md`.

## Knots and lashings

Each numbered instruction in `06-knots.txt` has its own image immediately below it. `vector-manifest.json` records the source and construction check for each of the 52 diagrams. Review considered the rope route, which strand lies above each crossing, where the working end goes, and how the ends finish. Paper gaps distinguish the rope below a crossing. Poles hide rope passing behind them; lashing drawings are front-view schematics, with finishing-hitch insets where the main view would hide the hitch.

|---|---|---|

Animations and stills were inspected in the ignored `art/reference/` folder, including BALOO's illustrated taut-line/bowline page. Review downloads stay outside the app's assets. Do not infer rope strength, tail length, load rating, or approval for climbing/rescue from the artwork. The app's bowline text now explains slack-load loosening and the existing shear article distinguishes a separate three-pole tripod lashing.
